FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is AmaazLoader?

AmaazLoader is a Windows utility designed to sign and install IPA files on an iPhone or iPad using your own Apple Account.

Does AmaazLoader save my Apple Account password?

AmaazLoader does not intentionally save your Apple Account password or two-factor authentication code. Credentials are supplied when required during authentication.

What do I need?

A 64-bit Windows PC, an iPhone or iPad connected by USB, an Apple Account, and an internet connection for Apple authentication and signing services.

Does it support two-factor authentication?

Yes. AmaazLoader includes a dedicated verification-code flow when Apple requests two-factor authentication.
